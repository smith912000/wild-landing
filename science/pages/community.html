Frequently asked

Questions about joining.

Do I get access to both Discord servers, or just one?
Both. Whether you join via the £10/mo sub or via a course purchase, you have access to both the Oneirology and The Dreaming Path Discords. Hop between them, settle into one — your choice.
How does the free first month work?
For a limited time at launch, you can join both Discords completely free for 30 days. No card required. After 30 days, access pauses unless you start a £10/mo sub or buy a course tier. We'll send one reminder before access pauses. Cancel anytime if you don't want to continue.
If I buy a course later, do I get the £10/mo I've already paid back?
We'll credit up to your first 3 months of Discord sub against any course tier purchase. Email us with your sub start date and we'll send a discount code at checkout.
What's the difference between the weekly Q&A and the private DM coaching?
Weekly Q&A is a group session on Discord, open to Tier 2 and Tier 3 buyers, recorded if you can't attend live. Private DM coaching is one-to-one — message a coach directly with your specific question, get a precise response back. DM access is Tier 3 only.
Can I switch between Discord servers later?
There's no switching — you have access to both. Many members are active in one and lurk the other. Some are equally active in both. Up to you.
Is the community moderated?
Yes. Both Discords have founder + team presence daily. No spam, no pseudoscience drift, no recruitment for unrelated projects. Different vocabularies (science vs contemplative) are welcomed but kept in their own tracks so neither dilutes the other.
Cancel anytime?
Yes. The £10/mo sub is cancellable anytime from your account page. No retention friction, no win-back emails. Access ends at the end of your current billing month.
